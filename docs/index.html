How Balloon works

Three steps, run on your own device every time a post appears. Nothing you browse ever leaves your browser.

1
🔎

Detect

Matches the author against a curated list of state-affiliated, state-funded and low-credibility accounts, including their aliases across platforms.

2
🔗

Verify

Unwraps redirect links (t.co, l.facebook.com, YouTube redirects) and checks the real destination against a list of known bad sources.

3
🎈

Decide

Flagged posts get a badge and a cited source strip. Posts you've chosen to block are hidden behind an overlay you can open with one click.

🏷️
Cited warnings

Every badge links its evidence: EU sanctions, Ofcom rulings, FARA filings and more.

🚫
Keyword blocking

Hide posts containing any word or phrase, on every platform at once.

🌐
Website blocking

Right-click any link to block its website, or block posts linking to bad sources.

✅
Your call

Trust an account, disable a rule, or export your settings. Nothing is hidden for good.
