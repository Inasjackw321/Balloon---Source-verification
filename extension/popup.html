Warning categories

★ State-Affiliated Government-controlled with no editorial independence. Worst cases have content blocked.
◉ State-Funded Funded by a government; may retain partial independence but serves state interests.
▲ Bad Source Consistently low accuracy, partisan fringe, or documented misinformation track record.

Methodology & sources

Accounts and websites are flagged based on foreign-agent registrations (FARA, EU), broadcast licence actions (Ofcom, FCC), sanctions (OFAC, EU), academic research (Oxford Internet Institute, Stanford Internet Observatory) and fact-checking databases. Hover any badge or source chip for its citations.
